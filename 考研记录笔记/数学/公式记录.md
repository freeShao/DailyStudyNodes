$sinx = x - \frac{1}{3!}x^3 + \frac{1}{5!}x^5 + o(x^5)$ 
$cosx = 1 - \frac{1}{2!}x^2 = \frac{1}{4!}x^4 + o(x^4)$

## 3.1. 积分化简手法
### 3.1.1. 拆项

#### （一） 分母有什么 分子拆什么（方便约分）

$\frac{t^2}{t^2-1} = \frac{t^2 \color{blue} -1+1}{t^2-1}=1+\frac{1}{t^2-1}$ 

$\frac{1}{1+e^x} = \frac{1 \color{blue} +e^x-e^x}{1+e^x}=1-\frac{e^x}{1+e^x}$ 

$\frac{1}{x(1+x)}=\frac{1 \color{blue} +x-x}{x(1+x)}=\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{1+x}$ 



$$\left.\begin{array}{l} 
\frac{1}{x^{2}\left(1+x^{2}\right)}=\frac{1 \color{blue} +x^{2}-x^{2}}{x^{2}\left(1+x^{2}\right)}=\frac{1}{x^{2}}-\frac{1}{1+x^{2}} \\
\frac{1}{t(1+t)}=\frac{1 \color{blue} +t-t}{t(1+t)}=\frac{1}{t}-\frac{1}{1+t} \\
\frac{1}{e^{x}\left(1+e^{x}\right)}=\frac{1 \color{blue} +e^{x}-e^{x}}{e^{x}\left(1+e^{x}\right)}=\frac{1}{e^{x}}-\frac{1}{1+e^{x}}
\end{array}\right\} \rightarrow \color{red}{\frac{1}{n(n+1)}=\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+1}}$$ 

$\frac{1}{t(t-1)}=\frac{ \color{blue} t-(t-1)}{t(t-1)}=\frac{1}{t-1}-\frac{1}{t} \rightarrow \color{red}{\frac{1}{n(n-1)}=\frac{1}{n-1}-\frac{1}{n}}$ 



$\frac{1}{x^7(x^7+2)} = {\color{blue}\frac{1}{2}} \cdot \frac{{\color{blue}x^7+2-x^7}}{x^7(x^7+2)} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{x^7}-\frac{1}{x^7+2}\right) \rightarrow \color{red}{\frac{1}{n(n+k)} = \frac{1}{k}\left(\frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+k}\right)}$ 

$\frac{1}{x(x^7+2)} = {\color{blue}\frac{1}{2}} \cdot \frac{{\color{blue}x^7+2-x^7}}{x(x^7+2)} = \frac{1}{2x} - \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{x^6}{x^7+2}$ 



$\frac{1}{x^4(1-x^4)} = \frac{\color{blue}{1-x^4+x^4}}{x^4(1-x^4)} = \frac{1}{x^4} + \frac{1}{1-x^4}$ 

$\frac{1}{x^2(1-x^4)} = \frac{\color{blue}{1-x^4+x^4}}{x^2(1-x^4)} = \frac{1}{x^2} + \frac{x^2}{\color{red}{1-x^4}}$



$\frac{x^2}{1-x^4} = \frac{x^2}{(1+x^2)(1-x^2)} = {\color{blue}\frac{1}{2}} \cdot \frac{{\color{blue}1+}x^2 \color{blue} -{(1-x^2)}}{(1+x^2)(1-x^2)} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{1-x^2} - \frac{1}{1+x^2}\right)$ 

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{x^2}{1-x^4} = \frac{x^2 {\color{blue}+1-1}}{(1+x^2)(1-x^2)} = \frac{1}{1-x^2} - \frac{1}{(1+x^2)(1-x^2)} &= \frac{1}{1-x^2} - {\color{blue}\frac{1}{2}} \cdot \frac{{\color{blue}1+x^2+1-x^2}}{(1+x^2)(1-x^2)} \\ &= \frac{1}{1-x^2} - \frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{1-x^2} - \frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{1+x^2} \\ &= \frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{1-x^2} - \frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{1+x^2}
\end{aligned}$$ 
$\color{orange}{\frac{1}{(1 + \boxed{\phantom{a}})(1 - \boxed{\phantom{a}}) } = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{1 + \boxed{\phantom{a}}} + \frac{1}{1 - \boxed{\phantom{a}}}\right)}$ 


$\frac{1}{t^2-1} = \frac{1}{(t+1)(t-1)} = {\color{blue}\frac{1}{2}} \cdot \frac{{\color{blue}t+1-(t-1)}}{(t+1)(t-1)} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{t-1} - \frac{1}{t+1}\right)$ 

$\frac{1}{1-t^2} = \frac{1}{(1+t)(1-t)} = {\color{blue}\frac{1}{2}} \cdot \frac{{\color{blue}1+t+1-t}}{(1+t)(1-t)} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{1+t} + \frac{1}{1-t}\right)$ 

$\frac{1}{(t^2-1)(1+t)} = {\color{blue}\frac{1}{2}} \cdot \frac{1 {\color{blue}+t+1-t}}{(t^2-1)(1+t)} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{t^2-1} - \frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{(1+t)^2}$ 

$\frac{e^x}{\sqrt{e^x-1}} = \frac{e^x {\color{blue}-1+1}}{\sqrt{e^x-1}} = \sqrt{e^x-1} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{e^x-1}}$ 

$\frac{2t^2}{t^2+1} = \frac{2t^2 {\color{blue}+2-2}}{t^2+1} = 2 - \frac{2}{t^2+1}$

### 3.1.2. 提项

#### （一） 提高次（外面提里面除）

$\frac{1}{x(x^7 + 2)} = \frac{1}{x^8 (1+2x^{-7})}$ 

#### （二） 提根号内的一项

$\begin{array}{ll} \frac{1}{\sqrt{x(4-x)}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{x} \cdot \sqrt{(4-x)}} & \frac{1}{x \sqrt{(x^2-1)}} = \frac{1}{x^2 \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{x^2}}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{x-x^2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{x} \cdot \sqrt{1-x}} & \frac{1}{x \sqrt{(1+x^2)}} = \frac{1}{x^2 \sqrt{1+\frac{1}{x^2}}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2-x}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{x} \cdot \sqrt{x-1}} \end{array}$ 

#### （三） 提 $e^x$
$\frac{1}{1+ {\color{blue} e^x}} = \frac{1}{e^x(e^{-x}+1)}$ 
$\frac{1}{1-e^{2x}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{e^2x(e^{-2x}-1)}} = \frac{1}{e^x \sqrt{e^{-2x}-1}}$ 
#### （四） 分子比分母高几次就提几次，然后拆项
$\frac{x^3}{x^2 + 1} = \frac{x(x^2+1) - x}{x^2 + 1} = x - \frac{x}{x^2 + 1}$ 

### 3.1.3. 同乘 - 同除同理
#### （一） 多项式差几次乘几次

#### （二） 利用平方差公式
**三角函数化简**
$\frac{1}{1-sinx} = \frac{\color{blue}{1+sinx}}{(1-sinx)\color{blue}{(1+sinx)}} = \frac{1+sinx}{1-sin^2x} = \frac{1+sinx}{cox^2x} = sec^2x + \frac{sinx}{cos^2x}$ 
**分母有理化**
$\frac{x^2}{1+\sqrt{1-x^2}} = \frac{x^2\color{blue}{(1-\sqrt{1-x^2})}}{(1+\sqrt{1-x^2})}$ 
#### （三） 同乘 $e^x$
$\begin{array}{ll} \frac{1}{1+e^x} = \frac{e^x}{e^x(1+e^x)} & \frac{1}{e^x + e^(2x)} = \frac{e^x}{e^(2x) + e^2} \end{array}$
